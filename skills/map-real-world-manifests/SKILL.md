# Map Real-World Manifests

## Purpose
- Model real-world entities as manifests: places, automaton/agent locations, work items, resources, and routes.
- Keep coordinates and manifests grounded in real/configured/observed sources; never invent locations.
- Surface spatial context in UI/API and link manifests to automaton states, runs, and reports.

## Operations
- Record/update places and automaton locations with source and timestamp.
- Track work items and resources tied to locations/places.
- Plan/quantify routes with explicit estimates vs confirmed values.
- Relate manifests to live operations (paywall, settlement, funding, execution) where applicable.
